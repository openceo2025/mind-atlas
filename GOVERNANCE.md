# Mind Atlas governance

Mind Atlas is currently maintained by `openceo2025`, the owner of the official
repository at <https://github.com/openceo2025/mind-atlas>.

## Decision making

The Project Owner is the final decision maker for releases, roadmap, security,
licensing, trademarks, commercial agreements, and acceptance of contributions.
Maintainers may delegate review and implementation authority without
transferring ownership of the project or its marks.

Community discussion and pull requests are encouraged. Technical decisions
should be explained in issues, pull requests, release notes, or repository
documentation when they materially affect contributors or users.

## Contributions

External contributions require acceptance of [CLA.md](CLA.md). Contributors
retain copyright while granting the Project Owner the rights described in the
CLA. Accepted contributions are published in the community repository under
`AGPL-3.0-only`, unless a file contains an explicit different notice.

## Project assets

The project assets that should be kept transferable together include:

- the official repository and release history;
- contributor and CLA records;
- the `mind-atlas.org` domain and website configuration;
- Mind Atlas names, logos, social accounts, and other brand assets;
- commercial license agreements and customer records;
- separately licensed Pro, Sync, Team, packaging, and hosted-service code;
- documentation, analytics, and operational accounts required to run official
  services.

## Organization migration and succession

Before adding multiple maintainers or entering commercial contracts, the
Project Owner should create a dedicated GitHub organization and transfer the
official repository into it. GitHub's transfer process preserves repository
history, issues, pull requests, stars, watchers, and forks.

The organization name, legal entity, owner roles, recovery methods, billing,
domain control, and trademark ownership must be chosen explicitly before the
transfer. Repository transfer must not be performed to an unspecified or
uncontrolled organization.

Any successor or acquirer must preserve existing open-source grants. The
Project Owner may transfer the project, commercial rights, trademarks, and CLA
rights to a successor or acquirer subject to applicable agreements and law.

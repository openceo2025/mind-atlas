# Mind Atlas Promotion Operations

Status: canonical promotion plan

Last strategy decision: 2026-07-16

This document is the source of truth for promotion work. The append-only
experiment record is `docs/promotion-experiments.md`. Update both after every
completed promotion loop.

## 1. Objective and non-goals

Primary objective:

- Grow free Google-linked users from the current baseline to 100, 1,000, and
  10,000.

Quality guardrails:

- A new Google-linked user should save an atlas to cloud within 24 hours.
- A useful share of each acquisition cohort should return within seven days.
- Promotion must minimize owner time as well as cash CPA.

Non-goals:

- Do not optimize for paid subscriptions in this promotion loop.
- Do not use `first_node_created` as the promotion success metric.
- Do not describe hosted free mode as Codex, Claude Code, or OpenClaw mission
  control. Those capabilities are local-only, while hosted AI is paid.
- Do not use novel writing, worldbuilding, or TRPG as the primary target. That
  previous direction was explicitly rejected on 2026-07-16.
- Do not buy broad traffic before campaign-to-Google attribution works.

## 2. Positioning

Primary audience:

- Young adults, approximately 18-34, who keep everyday notes and tasks in
  Windows Sticky Notes or similar loose-note tools.
- Students and early-career workers handling study, work, personal projects,
  and side projects at the same time.
- Productivity, desk-setup, digital-organization, and information-overload
  audiences. Do not infer or target a medical diagnosis.
- Independent VTubers and other streamers managing broadcasts, thumbnails,
  clips, collaborations, sponsorships, and private tasks in parallel.

Primary promise:

> What if your sticky notes became a universe?

Supporting promise:

> Tasks, ideas, and plans stop covering the desktop and become one visual
> space that can be saved free with Google.

Important truth-in-advertising boundary:

- Mind Atlas does not currently synchronize directly with Windows Sticky
  Notes. A video may show copying note text into the existing text-outline
  import, but it must not claim automatic Sticky Notes import or sync.

The anonymous blank notebook remains available. Google sign-in should be
requested at the high-intent moment when the user wants to save, copy, or
remix an atlas.

## 3. Growth engine

The promotion system has four stages. They are one pipeline, not independent
campaigns.

1. One real-product vertical video demonstrates sticky-note chaos becoming a
   Mind Atlas universe.
2. Productivity and young-business creators apply the same transformation to
   their real workflow.
3. VTubers apply it to a real week of stream preparation and multitasking.
4. Acquired users publish or share useful atlases, creating the referral loop
   required for growth beyond 1,000 users.

The conversion path is:

`short video -> campaign URL -> real product value -> Google save -> return or share`

## 4. Short-video production decision

Use CapCut Desktop as a semi-automatic editor.

Do not use a fully generated text-to-video advertisement. Generative video can
invent the interface and tends to look like generic AI advertising. A person
selects four accurate Mind Atlas screen clips; CapCut handles cutting, timing,
captions, voice, reframing, and language variants.

The first master asset is:

- Asset id: `sticky_master_01`
- Length: 18-20 seconds
- Frame: 9:16, 1080 x 1920
- Export: H.264 MP4, 30 fps
- Captions: burned in so the message works with sound off

Storyboard:

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-1.8 s | A Windows desktop covered with Sticky Notes | `POV: your desktop has 37 sticky notes` |
| 1.8-4.0 s | Real note text copied into text-outline import | `Copy. Paste.` |
| 4.0-9.0 s | Nodes reform into the strongest Mind Atlas view | `Your notes become a universe.` |
| 9.0-13.0 s | Edit a node and switch layout | `Tasks - ideas - plans` |
| 13.0-16.0 s | Real hosted cloud-save action | `Free - save with Google` |
| 16.0-19.0 s | Clean product view and URL | `mind-atlas.org` |

Production workflow:

1. Codex prepares the demo atlas, exact four-shot list, caption file, and link.
2. The owner records four real UI clips. Target owner time: 15 minutes.
3. Import the clips into one reusable CapCut Desktop project.
4. Use automatic cutting, captioning, voice, and reframing; correct only factual
   errors and bad cuts.
5. Export one silent-safe master. Change platform caption and first comment,
   not the master video.
6. After three validated masters, use Metricool to schedule YouTube Shorts,
   TikTok, Instagram Reels, X, and LinkedIn from one calendar.

Expected effort:

- First master: 30-40 minutes of human editing.
- Later videos from the template: 10-15 minutes.
- Language variant: 5-10 minutes to verify captions, CTA, and proper nouns.

Commercial-use rule:

- Use only owned UI footage, owned voice, and assets explicitly licensed for
  commercial use.
- Do not use CapCut Commercial Sounds in the cross-platform master. Their
  license is limited to CapCut and TikTok surfaces. Use owned audio, silence
  plus UI sound effects, or music with an explicit all-social commercial
  license.

References:

- CapCut Auto Cut: https://www.capcut.com/help/auto-cut-in-capcut
- CapCut captions: https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles
- CapCut Auto Reframe: https://www.capcut.com/tools/auto-reframe
- CapCut commercial-use guidance: https://www.capcut.com/trust/legal

## 6. Creator strategy

### 6.1 Young-business and productivity creators

Do not target generic business celebrities. Select creators whose recent work
already discusses Windows desk setup, Sticky Notes, study and work systems,
digital organization, information overload, multiple projects, or side work.

Selection rules:

- Audience concentrated in adults age 18-34
- Median relevant-video views from 5,000 to 50,000 over the latest ten videos
- Sponsored posts normally retain at least half of normal median views
- Sponsored content is no more than roughly one in three posts
- The creator can record an understandable screen workflow
- Follower and view patterns do not look manipulated

Deliverable:

- One disclosed 25-40 second vertical video using the creator's real tasks
- One unique campaign link in the first comment, description, or profile
- Seven days of publication
- No scripted praise; honest limitations are allowed

Initial offer, used as a test rather than a market-rate claim:

- JPY 8,000 fixed
- JPY 200 per valid referred user
- Maximum 50 performance users

For both creator types, a valid referred user is an attributed, unique,
newly-created Google-linked user. Returning users, duplicate attribution, and
obvious abuse do not qualify. Cloud save and seven-day return remain product
quality metrics; creator payment does not depend on defects or friction in the
product after Google sign-in.

### 6.2 VTubers

Start with adult independent VTubers or small agencies, not major agencies.
Major agencies are slower, more expensive, and require formal approval before
this funnel is proven.

Selection rules:

- Adult independent creator or small agency
- 10,000-150,000 subscribers
- At least three streams per week
- Chatting, work, art, editing, programming, or production content
- Typical live concurrency around 100-1,500
- A public business contact exists
- The audience is primarily adult

Deliverable:

- An 8-12 minute disclosed segment organizing one real production week
- A 25-40 second Short cut from that segment
- Branches such as streams, thumbnails, clips, collaborations, sponsors, and
  private work
- A copyable `Streamer Week` template after the product supports that path
- Private sponsors, Discord messages, contact details, and unreleased plans are
  replaced with dummy data
- Mind Atlas receives no right to train on or synthesize the VTuber's voice or
  model

Initial offer:

- JPY 12,000 fixed
- JPY 200 per valid referred user
- Maximum 75 performance users

### 6.3 First paid pilot

The current JPY 10,000 budget is a discovery pilot, not platform ad spend:

- One Japanese productivity micro-creator
- JPY 8,000 fixed plus JPY 200 per G, capped at 10 G
- JPY 10,000 maximum total
- One disclosed real-workflow Short and one trackable link

This discovery pilot may start after attribution is verified even if owned
accounts cannot produce a statistically useful landing sample. Its purpose is
to acquire the first relevant-audience sample. Do not use X paid promotion for
this first JPY 10,000; the existing X audiences are topic-mismatched, while a
carefully selected creator supplies both relevance and credible demonstration.

Do not contact every country at once. After the discovery result passes the
scale gate, run two clean expansion batches:

- Business batch: two Japanese creators and one English creator
- VTuber batch: one Japanese VTuber and one English VTuber

Maximum test exposure:

- Discovery plus two remaining business pilots: JPY 46,000
- Two VTuber pilots: JPY 54,000
- Total maximum: JPY 100,000

Do not release the expansion budget until combined owned and discovery traffic
proves at least 8 percent campaign-landing to new-Google conversion with at
least 20 G. If the budget remains JPY 10,000, stop after the discovery pilot
and its seven-day decision. Do not pretend that a five-creator paid pilot fits
that budget.

After Japanese and English results, expand only the winning creator type:

1. Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Indonesian, and Korean
2. French, German, Hindi, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic

Simplified Chinese traffic is not treated as mainland-China demand while a
Google account is required. Traditional Chinese promotion should focus on
Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, and overseas Chinese audiences.

Use TikTok One and Instagram Creator Marketplace for scaled business-creator
prospecting only after the first five manual pilots. Continue direct public
business-email outreach for VTubers.

## 8. Ethics and disclosure contract

Do not exploit fan loyalty or request disguised endorsements. That behavior
creates low-intent accounts, creator risk, regulatory risk, and long-term brand
damage.

Every paid or rewarded post must:

- Use the platform paid-promotion control where available
- State `Ad`, `PR`, or `Sponsored by Mind Atlas` in the content language
- Put the disclosure in the video and post text, not behind a click
- For live video, repeat the disclosure at reasonable intervals
- Reflect real use; do not require claims the creator cannot honestly make

Official references:

- US FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers
- Japan Consumer Affairs Agency: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/faq/stealth_marketing/
- YouTube: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235
- TikTok: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service
- Instagram: https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1109894795810258/
- X: https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/paid-partnerships-policy
- European Commission: https://commission.europa.eu/topics/consumers/consumer-rights-and-complaints/influencer-legal-hub_en

## 9. Attribution engineering gate

No paid creator campaign starts until these hosted-only changes are deployed
and verified:

1. Add short campaign URLs such as `/go/{campaign}/{partner}/{asset}`.
2. Preserve a sanitized campaign, partner, asset, locale, and platform code
   through Google OAuth.
3. Record `google_user_created` only when a user row is newly created; do not
   treat every OAuth completion as a new acquisition.
4. Add an allowlisted `trigger` to `google_login_started`, such as `cloud_save`,
   `share`, `account`, or `remix`.
5. Add cumulative Google-user progress, campaign attribution, 24-hour cloud
   save, Google-user D1/D7/D30, and campaign CPA inputs to `growth-report`.
6. Preserve UTM parameters from localized About pages if those pages are ever
   used. Until then, campaigns link directly to the app or a dedicated sticky
   notes landing experience.
7. Re-verify client analytics coverage. Conversion rates are not trusted while
   coverage remains near one percent.

Platform views, completion rate, and engagement are entered manually at 24
hours, 72 hours, and seven days. Do not build platform API integrations before
manual reporting becomes burdensome.

Campaign-link format:

`https://mind-atlas.org/go/{campaign}/{partner}/{asset}?platform={platform}&locale={locale}`

Codes use ASCII letters, digits, `_`, or `-`. Example for the first Japanese X
post:

`https://mind-atlas.org/go/sticky_2026w30_01_owned_short/openceo2025/sticky_master_01?platform=x&locale=ja`

`growth-report` exposes campaign landings, Google login starts/completions,
new Google users, 24-hour cloud saves, and age-eligible D1/D7/D30 retention.
Cash spend and owner minutes remain manual experiment-ledger inputs so private
commercial records are not added to product analytics.

## 10. KPI definitions

Primary metric:

- `G`: newly created Google-linked users attributed to the experiment

Quality metrics:

- `Q`: new Google users who complete cloud save within 24 hours
- `R7`: new Google users with a meaningful edit or cloud save around day 7

Cost and effort:

- `CPA-G = campaign cash spend / G`
- `effective CPA = (cash spend + owner minutes / 60 * JPY 3,000) / G`

The owner-time rate is a planning convention, not an accounting claim. Report
cash CPA and owner minutes separately as well.

Initial funnel hypothesis:

- Video view to campaign landing: 1 percent
- Campaign landing to new Google user: 8 percent
- New Google user to 24-hour cloud save: 40 percent
- One new Google user per 1,250 video views

Milestone model at that baseline:

| Goal | Video views | Campaign landings | Expected Q |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 G | 125,000 | 1,250 | 40 |
| 1,000 G | 1,250,000 | 12,500 | 400 |
| 10,000 G | 12,500,000 | 125,000 | 4,000 |

A strong-fit creator scenario of 1.5 percent view-to-landing and 10 percent
landing-to-G produces one new Google user per about 667 views. Treat both
models as hypotheses until the first five creator pilots provide real data.

Milestone channel roles:

- 0-100: owned master videos plus the first five creator pilots
- 100-1,000: repeat only creator types with CPA-G at or below JPY 300
- 1,000-10,000: creator campaigns become seeds; the product share/referral
  loop must provide compounding growth

Referral coefficient:

`K = share rate * shares per sharer * viewers per share * viewer-to-new-G rate`

At 1,000 users:

- If `K < 0.3`, do not buy creator campaigns at large scale. Fix sharing.
- Target a blended CPA-G of JPY 150 or less for the path to 10,000.

## 11. Weekly execution and review loop

Change one primary variable per week.

- Week 0: deploy and verify attribution engineering
- Week 1: publish `sticky_master_01` from owned accounts
- Week 2: publish one JPY 10,000 Japanese productivity-creator discovery pilot
- Week 3: if the scale gate passes and a larger budget is approved, publish
  the remaining business-creator pilots
- Week 4: publish the two VTuber pilots only after the business batch is
  measured
- Week 5: repeat only the winning audience, hook, and format

Weekly routine:

- Monday: save 7-day and 30-day VPS baselines; freeze hypothesis, audience,
  asset, and budget
- Tuesday: publish the owned asset or creator pilot
- Wednesday: enter 24-hour platform metrics
- Friday: enter 72-hour metrics; change nothing except technical failures
- Following Tuesday: enter seven-day metrics and decide `scale`, `revise`, or
  `stop`

Do not mix business creators and VTubers in the same experiment batch. Use
separate source codes for VTuber live segment, Short, community post, and
profile link.

Scale gate:

- View to landing at least 0.8 percent
- Landing to G at least 8 percent
- OAuth start to completion at least 80 percent
- G to Q at least 40 percent
- R7 / G at least 15 percent
- At least 20 new Google users in the sample
- CPA-G inside the current milestone ceiling

Initial CPA-G ceilings:

- First 100: JPY 500 target after the learning pilot
- To 1,000: JPY 300
- To 10,000: JPY 150 blended

Revise or stop:

- After 10,000 views, fewer than 30 campaign landings: replace hook or CTA
- After 200 landings, fewer than 6 new Google users: fix landing and save value
- After 50 OAuth starts, fewer than 40 completions: fix authentication flow
- After 20 G, fewer than 5 Q: stop the promise or first-save experience
- After 50 G, fewer than 5 R7: stop scaling that audience
- After at least 20 G from one creator, CPA above the milestone ceiling: do not
  rebook that creator type

Rate decisions with a denominator below 20 are directional only.

## 12. Owner-effort contract

The system is deliberately designed to reduce owner work.

Codex or OpenClaw can:

- prepare demo data, shot lists, scripts, captions, and localized copy
- research public creators and draft personalized outreach
- implement attribution and VPS reports
- calculate weekly results and recommend scale/revise/stop
- maintain the public aggregate experiment ledger

The owner is responsible only for actions requiring identity, taste, money, or
external authority:

- approve the final master video
- connect or authorize social accounts
- approve recipients and send terms
- approve spend and payments
- approve public posts and creator contracts

Private creator contact details, unreleased negotiations, credentials, and
individual contract terms must remain outside the public repository.
